# Power

| Board | Battery | Charge | Idle display | Peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cardputer ADV | 1750 mAh internal | USB-C / TP4057 | tens of mA | RF TX + backlight |
| ES3C28P | 3.7 V pack on 1.25 mm BAT | Type-C, ~290 mA | ~140 mA backlight | ~560 mA display+speaker+charge |

Two packs. No 5V loop. ADV side switch OFF while flashing.
Leave Grove Red unconnected.
